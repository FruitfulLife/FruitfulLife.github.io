About

In a world full of dupes why not at least be authentically you?

My Philosophy

I am committed to walking alongside you as discover who you are and heal from what’s gotten you there. I work to create a safe space that fosters trust, open communication, and understanding so that you can find what a fruitful life looks like to you and your unique needs.

Areas of Focus

I work with female adolescents (12+) through adults for a variety of concerns. My focus is on women specific issues such as post-partum depression, sexual trauma, post abortion healing, grief and loss, and PTSD. I also work with couples, relationship issues, anxiety, depression, parenting, and mental illnesses.

Education & Credentials

I have a master’s degree in clinical Mental Health Counseling from Houston Christian University and a Bachelor of Arts in Psychology from Texas A&M University. I am licensed in the State of Texas as a Licensed Professional Counselor.

About Me

When not working, I can be found spending meaningful time with my husband, chasing around and giggling with my toddler and 6-month-old, or watching reality TV. I love to catch up with friends over Tex-Mex or coffee and will never miss my morning latte or afternoon Dr. Pepper. I love all things lime green, doing my nails, and keeping up with the latest fashion trends.
