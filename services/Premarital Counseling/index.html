Premarital Counseling

Premarital counseling is an invaluable investment in your future marriage. This specialized counseling service helps couples prepare for the journey ahead by providing essential tools, insights, and communication skills that will strengthen your relationship from the very beginning.

Why Choose Premarital Counseling?

Marriage is one of life’s most significant commitments, and preparing for it thoughtfully can make all the difference. Premarital counseling allows you to:

  • Gain deeper insight into your partner that you may not have discussed before
  • Build courage to communicate openly about important topics related to marriage
  • Work through potential challenges before they become obstacles
  • Fortify your relationship for the lifelong journey ahead
  • Establish healthy communication patterns that will serve you throughout your marriage

Our Approach

At Fruitful Life Counseling, we use the proven SYMBIS (Saving Your Marriage Before It Starts) curriculum, a comprehensive program designed specifically for engaged couples. This evidence-based approach covers essential topics that every couple should address before saying “I do.”

Program Details

Format: Eight one-hour counseling sessions
Setting: Individual couple sessions with Licensed Professional Counselor Brooke Johnson
Curriculum: SYMBIS (Saving Your Marriage Before It Starts)
Investment: Regular session fees apply

Special Benefit for Texas Couples

Upon successful completion of all eight sessions, you will receive a certificate that can be used to obtain your marriage license free of charge in the state of Texas, providing additional value to your investment in your relationship.

What to Expect

Each session is designed to help you explore important aspects of your relationship in a safe, supportive environment. You’ll have the opportunity to discuss topics that are crucial for marital success, practice healthy communication skills, and address any concerns or questions about your upcoming marriage.

Ready to Begin?

Investing in premarital counseling is investing in your future happiness together. If you’re ready to strengthen your relationship and prepare for a successful marriage, reach out through our contact form to inquire about scheduling your premarital counseling sessions.

Premarital counseling is a way to help prepare yourself for marriage. It’s a great way to fortify your lifelong relationship and ensure you’re building on a strong foundation of understanding, communication, and mutual respect.
