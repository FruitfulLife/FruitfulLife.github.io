Grief & Loss

Grief is one of the most universal human experiences, yet it can feel incredibly isolating and overwhelming. Loss takes many forms, and each person’s journey through grief is unique. I provide compassionate, specialized support to help you navigate the complex emotions and challenges that accompany loss in all its forms.

Understanding Grief and Loss

Grief extends far beyond the death of a loved one, though that remains one of the most profound losses we can experience. Loss can come from a variety of life circumstances, and each type of loss deserves recognition, validation, and support.

Types of Loss We Address

I work with individuals experiencing grief from many different sources:

  • Loss of a loved one - death of family members, friends, or pets
  • Loss of life experiences - dreams that didn’t materialize, missed opportunities, or changed life plans
  • Loss of mobility or physical function - injury, illness, or age-related changes
  • Loss of relationships - divorce, separation, or estrangement
  • Loss of role or identity - career changes, retirement, or empty nest syndrome
  • Loss of health - chronic illness diagnosis or progressive conditions
  • Miscarriage or pregnancy loss
  • Loss of independence - due to illness, aging, or life circumstances
  • Any other significant loss that impacts your life and wellbeing

Specialized Support for Complex Grief

Some individuals experience prolonged or complicated grief that may require additional specialized support. I am trained to recognize and address complex grief patterns, helping you when grief feels stuck, overwhelming, or interferes significantly with daily functioning.

A Safe Space for Your Grief

Grief can be messy, unpredictable, and deeply personal. I provide a safe, non-judgmental environment where you can express your feelings freely—whether that’s sadness, anger, confusion, relief, or any combination of emotions. Your grief is valid, regardless of what others might think or say.

Honoring Your Loss

Part of the healing process involves finding meaningful ways to honor what or whom you’ve lost. Together, we can explore how to carry forward the love, memories, or significance of what you’ve lost while still moving forward in your life.

Support at Your Own Pace

Healing from grief happens on your own timeline, not according to anyone else’s expectations. I’m here to support you wherever you are in your grief journey, whether your loss is recent or happened years ago.

Ready to Begin Healing?

Seeking support for grief takes courage, especially when you’re already feeling vulnerable. If you’re ready to receive compassionate, professional support as you navigate loss, reach out through our contact form to schedule your consultation.

Grief is the price we pay for love, and both deserve to be honored. You don’t have to walk through this difficult time alone.
